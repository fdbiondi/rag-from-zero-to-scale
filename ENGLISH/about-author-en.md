# About the author

Federico Andrés Biondi is the credited author of *RAG from Zero to Scale*, a technical guide designed to make retrieval-augmented generation accessible to developers and teams building reliable products. The book follows a progressive approach: understand the fundamental components, create a local prototype, evaluate service providers, and identify the challenges of operating real systems in production. Its educational focus emphasizes reproducibility, cost awareness, traceable answers, and intentional infrastructure decisions. The goal is to help readers without previous RAG experience experiment hands-on, develop troubleshooting skills, and form independent architectural judgment. This description summarizes the purpose of the book; it does not assign unverified positions, projects, qualifications, or work experience to the author.
