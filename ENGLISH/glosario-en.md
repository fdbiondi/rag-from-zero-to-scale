# Glossary

- **RAG:** retrieval-augmented generation.
- **Embedding:** numerical vector representing text.
- **Chunk:** fragment of a source document.
- **Top-k:** number of retrieved candidates.
- **Recall@k:** fraction of relevant evidence among k results.
- **Reranker:** model that reorders retrieved candidates.
- **Tenant:** logical customer or organization with isolated access.
- **p95:** 95th percentile latency.
