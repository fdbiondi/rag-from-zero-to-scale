# Preface

Generative AI can produce convincing answers without knowing your organization's documents. This book teaches you to retrieve evidence before generating prose and to recognize where a prototype is no longer production-safe. Concepts, architectural decisions, and reproducible procedures are separated; always review current provider versions and contracts.

**Learning path:** one hour for concepts, two hours as a goal for the local prototype (download speeds vary), and at least a week for preparing a security-conscious deployment.
